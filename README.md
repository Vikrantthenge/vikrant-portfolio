## Capacity & Backlog Planner

### Operational Problem

A shared-services team expects 1,230 requests across five working days. The manager needs to choose which staffing plan can clear the Friday backlog while controlling weekly labour cost.

### Solution

An interactive staffing simulator compares four plans: current staffing, shifting regular hours, adding overtime, and shifting hours with targeted overtime. Users can change daily demand, paid hours, productivity, labour rates and backlog targets. The app tracks how work carries forward each day and identifies the lowest-cost plan among those entered that meet the selected targets.

### Simulated Scenario Results

| Staffing plan | Requests completed | Friday backlog | Weekly labour cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current staffing | 1,200 | 30 | ₹84,000 |
| Shift regular hours | 1,200 | 30 | ₹84,000 |
| Add 24 overtime hours | 1,230 | 0 | ₹96,600 |
| **Shift regular hours and add 6 Monday overtime hours** | **1,230** | **0** | **₹87,150** |

The targeted overtime plan cleared all **1,230 simulated requests** by Friday and cost **₹9,450 less** than the 24-hour overtime plan. It cost **₹3,150 more** than the current roster and cleared the 30 requests that roster left open.

### Transferable capabilities demonstrated

Workforce planning · Workload and backlog analysis · Scenario modelling · Labour cost analysis · Operational decision support

### Technology

`Python` · `Streamlit` · `Pandas` · `Plotly` · `Power BI`

🔗 **GitHub Repository**

🔗 **Power BI Report (PDF)**

# Airline Ground Turnaround Performance Case Study

## Monitoring turnaround variance, assumed SLA compliance and on-time performance

### Operational Problem

Ground turnaround depends on coordinated activities across stations, ground handlers and scheduled time bands. When turnaround exceeds a planned or assumed threshold, it may contribute to departure delays. Performance data can help identify where further investigation is warranted, but it does not by itself establish the root cause.

### Solution

An interactive operational dashboard for monitoring turnaround variance, assumed SLA compliance and on-time performance. It compares indicators across stations and ground handlers and lets users filter the simulated records by operational dimensions to explore performance patterns.

The same approach to comparing sites, suppliers, service levels and process variation can be applied in other operational environments, subject to the definitions and data available in each business.

### Key Indicators

The dashboard analyses **1,500 simulated flight-turnaround records** covering January–March 2026.

| Indicator | Result from simulated dataset |
|---|---:|
| SLA breach rate | Approximately 45.7% |
| On-time performance (OTP) | Approximately 74.7% |
| Average turnaround variance | +10.1 minutes |
| Records analysed | 1,500 |

These are descriptive results from the demonstration dataset, not verified results from a real airline operation.

### What the Case Study Demonstrates

- Monitoring turnaround variance and assumed SLA compliance
- Tracking on-time performance and departure-delay indicators
- Comparing performance across stations and ground handlers
- Filtering results by station, handler and scheduled time band
- Using KPI views to support further operational investigation
- Applying transferable methods for vendor, service-level and site-performance reviews

### Technology

`Python` · `Streamlit` · `Pandas` · `Plotly` · `CSV` · `Operations Performance Analytics`

# From Data to Decision

The common decision-support approach behind the portfolio is:

```text
OPERATIONAL DATA
       ↓
PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
       ↓
EXCEPTION / RISK IDENTIFICATION
       ↓
INVESTIGATION AND CONTEXT CHECK
       ↓
MANAGEMENT DECISION
       ↓
OPERATIONAL ACTION
       ↓
OUTCOME REVIEW
```

The applications demonstrate ways to organize and examine information. They do not independently prove root cause, recommend action without human review, or establish a measurable business outcome. Those conclusions require validated data, operational context and follow-through.
